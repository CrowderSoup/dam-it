# Playtest feedback form

Thank you for trying this unfinished game. Short, candid answers are the most
useful. Please do not include your name, email address, or other identifying
information.

1. What device, browser, and controls did you use (keyboard or gamepad)?
2. Had you played this game before? **Yes / No**
3. About how many minutes did you play?
4. How far did you get? **First resource / first dam piece / finished dam /
   finished Lodge / finished both garden spots / something else**
5. In your own words, what did you think you were trying to accomplish?
6. Where, if anywhere, did you feel stuck or wish the game had explained
   something?
7. What was the most enjoyable or satisfying moment?
8. What felt slow, repetitive, or unnecessary?
9. What changes did you notice in the environment after building the dam?
10. Based only on the game, what effect do you think a beaver can have on an
    ecosystem?
11. How interested would you be in continuing into another area? **1 (not at
    all) / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 (very interested)**
12. Did you encounter a control, audio, visual, performance, save, or browser
    problem? If so, what happened?

Optional: Is there anything else you expected the game to let you do?
