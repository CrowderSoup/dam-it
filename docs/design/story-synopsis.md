# One-Page Story Synopsis

Status: **Approved**  
Milestone: **0 — Vision lock**

## Opening — The quiet creek

Reed, a capable young beaver apprentice, leaves their family territory to build
a first independent home. They carry a letter from Grandma Hazel, who was born
at Willowbend and remembers its deep pools, abundant willow, and lively wetland.
Reed arrives to find the old dam complex breached, the creek diminished, and
no pond deep enough for a lodge. Hazel's family dispersed normally, but years
without beaver maintenance followed by heavy runoff from an upstream wildfire
drained the connected wetlands. Moss, a frog surviving beside the last shallow
pool, doubts that a newcomer—or one repaired dam—can change much.

## Act I — Willowbend

Reed gathers local material and repairs Willowbend's porous dam, initially to
make a private home. Water returns to irregular low ground, Moss's habitat
expands, and Reed begins a lodge. Eddy, a native Bonneville cutthroat trout,
reveals the first tradeoff: the pond creates valuable habitat, but an unfinished
flow path restricts movement and concentrates silt. Reed observes conditions
above and below the build and preserves a passable overflow. Eddy moves among
the new habitats, other fish and frogs return, and Moss begins to trust that
Reed will stay. During the celebration, muddy meltwater carries charred wood
downstream. Marnie, a duck from Aspen Meadow, asks Reed to help upstream. Before
Reed leaves, Bramble the raccoon steals a small bundle of building material.

## Act II — Aspen Meadow

Marnie correctly sees the need for water but assumes another large dam is the
answer. Clover objects: that plan would flood the rabbit's low warren, while
Saffron explains that a deep lake would replace needed flowering meadow. Reed
helps establish a high-ground warren and crossings, then builds an adjustable
water-control structure. The player chooses a deep central pool favored by
Marnie or braided shallow channels favored by Saffron. Both are healthy but
materially different configurations, changing routes, resources, resident
routines, optional work, and later surge tasks; the player may rebuild for a
modest cost. Marnie learns to listen, Clover begins planning for the whole
community, and Saffron claims a place as a valued seasonal neighbor. Bramble
scavenges again, but tracks show the stolen material is being used. The trail
and increasingly charred debris lead Reed toward Moraine Basin.

## Act III — Moraine Basin

Reed enters a mixed burn of surviving forest, blackened patches, new growth,
lingering snow, and unstable channels. Bramble's shelter has been damaged
repeatedly since the fire. Bramble acted from need but is not blameless: taking
community material worsened a vulnerable structure. Bramble acknowledges the
harm and repairs more than was taken. Reed helps establish a safe high-ground
den, while Bramble maps runoff paths and retrieves deadfall from steep ground.
Together they reject one enormous barrier and prepare a connected system of
small porous dams, braces, and reopened side channels that slow water, catch
some sediment, and give runoff safe floodplain space. Warm rain approaches as
the residents prepare all three regions.

## Climax and ending — The spring surge

The storm accelerates mountain snowmelt in a staged, recoverable sequence. Reed
moves through configuration-specific problems while residents handle other
repairs and rescues. Preparation changes the difficulty and visible aftermath,
but no outcome erases major progress or prevents the ending. The final task
returns Reed to Willowbend, where the private home survives because the whole
upstream system and community work together.

After the surge, the residents gather at Reed's completed lodge. A watershed
montage preserves the Aspen configuration and the Moraine burn scar while
showing continued recovery. Reed reads one last reply from Hazel, quietly tends
the dam at dawn, and watches the pond wake. Credits lead into free play, where
players can revisit residents, reconfigure Aspen Meadow, maintain structures,
and finish optional habitat work.
