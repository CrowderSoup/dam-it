# Construction and Progression

Status: **Approved**  
Milestone: **0 — Vision lock**

## Decision rules

- Fixed story builds are permanent milestones.
- Functional choices can be rebuilt later for a modest material cost after a
  clear preview.
- Residents remember the first functional choice while the world reflects the
  current configuration.
- Cosmetic improvements can be changed freely.
- No construction choice can lock story progress, remove a resident, or force
  a save restart.

## Construction verbs

The 1.0 scope allows 3–4 well-developed build types across the whole game.

| Verb | First introduced | Ecological effect | Later reuse | Decision type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dam | Willowbend | Place or repair porous wood-and-stone segments to raise water, form ponds, and reconnect floodplain | Small debris dams and repairs in every region | Fixed milestones with repairable components |
| Channel | Aspen Meadow | Open, deepen, or plug marked side channels to redirect flow | Reconfigure Aspen Meadow and spread Moraine Basin runoff into safe floodplain pockets | Reversible functional choice |
| Brace | Moraine Basin | Anchor logs and stone against vulnerable banks, structures, or debris paths; improve resilience and trap some sediment | Structure and bank repairs during the spring surge | Fixed preparation with repairable components |
| Improve | Willowbend | Build a staged home or habitat feature that changes how a resident uses the world | Lodge, warren, nesting island, den, crossings, and flower margins | Fixed functional improvement unless explicitly cosmetic |

All four verbs use the same context-sensitive interaction rather than a tool
wheel. Every Improve project must change a resident routine, route, resource,
or story state—not merely fill a progress bar. Cosmetic decorating is optional
and separate from the four core verbs.

Moraine Basin's challenge is a connected system, not one giant barrier. The
raccoon identifies runoff paths and recovers useful deadfall from steep ground;
the protagonist applies the dam and water-distribution skills learned in the
first two regions. Individual components remain vulnerable and repairable, but
the completed network is a permanent story milestone.

### Aspen Meadow configuration

The water-distribution structure can be rebuilt for a modest material cost.
Both states are healthy habitats with the same expected overall value; neither
is the optimal or morally correct answer.

| Consequence | Deep pool | Braided meadow |
|---|---|---|
| Landscape | Large pond, nesting island, narrow overflow | Shallow channels, wet-soil mosaic, broad spring bloom |
| Traversal | Direct swimming route; a former land path is submerged | More land access; several channels require crossings |
| Ecology | More open water and pond plants; less flowering meadow | More sedges, willows, flowers, and pollinator habitat; less open water |
| Residents | Duck occupies the center; butterfly uses a small flower margin | Butterfly ranges across the meadow; duck uses a modest side pool |
| Resources | More edible pond plants | Faster berry recovery through expanded pollinator habitat |
| Optional activity | Recover floating objects and improve the nesting island | Restore flower patches and create safe meadow crossings |
| Spring surge | One central spillway becomes an intense maintenance point | Several distributed channels create smaller repair and rescue tasks |
| Epilogue | A wide reflective pond dominates the region shot | A flowering channel mosaic dominates the region shot |

## Player upgrades

Upgrades must change capability or route choice; capacity increases alone are
not sufficient.

| Upgrade | Capability change | Story or region gate | Later use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Read the Water | Preview water extent, direction, and affected habitat at build sites | Earned through Moss's and Eddy's observations in Act I | Required to understand Aspen Meadow configurations and Moraine Basin runoff paths |
| Shared Routes | Use restored route markers for quick travel between regions and open resident-built land and water shortcuts | Earned through Marnie, Clover, and Saffron in Act II | Makes preparation, optional requests, reconfiguration, and free-play visits efficient |
| Team Haul | Call a nearby resident at marked sites to move or anchor deadfall Reed cannot handle alone | Earned by reconciling with Bramble in Act III | Builds Moraine Basin's distributed controls and supports community repairs during the spring surge |
| Reinforced Pouch | Increase wood, stone, and berry capacity once | Optional Willowbend lodge improvement | Reduces gathering trips but never gates a story build |

The three relationship capabilities unlock automatically through story
progress; there is no skill tree. Replace the demo's three escalating pouch
tiers with one optional Reinforced Pouch so progression does not become a loop
of gathering for larger inventory numbers.
